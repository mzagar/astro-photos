Deep Sky from Donja Stubica

Astro photos with a ZWO Seestar S50 Pro

I photograph deep sky objects from my backyard in Donja Stubica, Croatia — a small town in the Zagorje hills, about 30 km north of Zagreb. The telescope is a smart scope that fits in a backpack: I put it on the ground, pick a target in the phone app, and it finds and tracks the object on its own.

It sits on an alt-az mount, so there is no polar alignment and no equatorial wedge to fiddle with. Field rotation is handled in software during stacking. Each picture is a stack of many short exposures, stretched and denoised in the Seestar app — the watermark in the corner is added by the app itself, so the object, time and integration travel with the file.
