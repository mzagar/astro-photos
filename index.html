Backyard Deep Sky

Astro photos with a ZWO Seestar S50 Pro

I photograph deep sky objects from my backyard in Donja Stubica, Croatia, a small town in the Zagorje hills, about 30 km north of Zagreb. The telescope is a smart scope that fits in a backpack: I put it on the ground, pick a target in the phone app, and it finds and tracks the object on its own.

Each picture is a stack of many short exposures, stretched and denoised in the Seestar app, the watermark in the corner is added by the app itself, so the object, time and integration travel with the file.
